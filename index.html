Corporate Overview

RZW ROYAL GAMES S.R.L. operates as a leading enterprise in the Retail Trade sector in Bucuresti, Romania. Despite our specialized name, our primary operational model is structured around large-scale general merchandise distribution and warehouse club management.

We provide consumers and B2B clients with access to a vast array of goods. From high-end electronics and entertainment merchandise (the core of our "Royal Games" heritage) to everyday lifestyle products, we operate on a membership-driven warehouse model that guarantees value and quality.

RZW Royal Club App

To streamline the shopping experience in our supercenters and online, RZW ROYAL GAMES S.R.L. develops and maintains a proprietary e-commerce application published on the Google Play Store.

The RZW Royal Club app replaces physical membership cards. It allows members to scan barcodes in our warehouse aisles for instant pricing, access digital catalogs of our general merchandise, and manage home deliveries.

  • Digital Warehouse Membership Card (QR Integration).
  • In-store barcode scanner for product info and fast checkout.
  • Full e-commerce catalog for electronics, games, and general merchandise.
  • Push notifications for exclusive warehouse club restocks and deals.
